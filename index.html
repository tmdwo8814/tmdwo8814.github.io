SJ Kim

I am an M.S. candidate at Kyung Hee University's Visual Science Lab (VSLab), advised by Prof. MyeongAh Cho.

My research centers on video understanding and 3D perception for intelligent systems. I focus on multi-object tracking with adaptive motion prediction, multi-modal 3D object detection through sensor fusion, and novel view synthesis using Neural Radiance Fields and 3D Gaussian Splatting. I am also interested in transfer learning approaches, including few-shot object detection and cross-domain adaptation.

My work aims to advance robust and generalizable computer vision technologies for understanding dynamic 3D environments in real-world applications.
